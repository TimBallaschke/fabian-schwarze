Projecttext:

With the money we collected from selling beer, we rented a Miles and drove to the hardware 
store to buy a few hundred euros worth of concrete. We got debris from everywhere;
Chris mother still had some in her garden. The other half consists of beer cans and trash. 
We build these places even though it is clear that they will be torn down again.

This city is not for the people who live in it, but for profit and a polished image.
So what is really left, if not taking back the city you live in?
We do it with concrete, in places where rats live and there is enough trash to fill ramps.
Places that have somehow been forgotten, creating space for something to emerge from the dirt.

Since 2023, I have been photographing my friends building these ramps.
