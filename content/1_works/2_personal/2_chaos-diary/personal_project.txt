Projecttext:

I think I would have liked to become a painter, a writer, or a singer, but I can't paint, and my words never felt strong enough. So photography became the way to tell this. 
It began with a fear of forgetting, of losing time, memories, or parts of myself.
That feeling has not really gone away, and in many ways this work still grows from it.

What remains is a collection shaped by fragments rather than order. There is no clear narrative and no chronology, just moments that felt important enough to hold on to.
Over time, these images have also become a reflection of the chaos that has always been part of my life, something I both embrace and resist.

All photographs shown here were taken between 2015 and today.
